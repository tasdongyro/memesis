# Image attribution

The pictures in this folder are the well-known meme originals, downscaled to at most 640 px and re-encoded.
They are used as small, non-commercial illustrations in a README, in the tradition of meme reuse. None of
them is part of the language or the runtime. If you hold rights to one of them and want it removed, open an
issue and it will be taken down.
